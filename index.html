01 · Brand Overview

Who We Are &
What We Stand For

Paruto is the parent brand behind a family of companies — Paruto Media, Paruto Music and Paruto Capital — and the products we build under the Paruto name. Each company has its own craft and its own audience. All of them share one standard.

The new Paruto identity brings that family together under a single mark: the interlocking P and D, set in gold on black. Wherever it appears, it should signal the same thing — ambition, quality and a commitment to excellence.

Purpose

To open new possibilities for the people, organisations and communities we serve

Positioning

A Nigerian-founded group building media, music, finance and technology ventures

Voice

Confident, optimistic, precise — never loud, never generic

"Think Possibilities" is not a slogan — it is how we work. Before we ask what is practical, we ask what is possible. Then we hold ourselves to the standard it takes to get there.

02 · Logo Story

The Mark & Its
Meaning

PPARUTO
+
DDOMINANCE

The Paruto symbol is an interlocking P and D — standing for Paruto and Dominance.

It is drawn as one continuous line. The P is the name; the D is the declaration — a commitment to lead in every space Paruto enters. Neither letter can be lifted out without breaking the other.

The forward curve at the base echoes a speech bubble and an arrow: ideas expressed with direction and intent. It is the same mark across every Paruto company — only the name beside it changes.

P

Paruto

The name — the people, the promise. Every engagement begins and ends with the Paruto standard.

D

Dominance

The ambition — to lead and define every space we enter, in Africa and beyond.

↗

Direction

The forward curve — a speech bubble and an arrow. Momentum, delivered outward.

04 · Logo System

Four Versions,
One Brand

Every logo file is vector artwork with a transparent background. Each version is designed for a specific background. Never create additional colour variations.

Gold — Primary Signature

On black and very dark backgrounds. The hero version, and first choice wherever the background permits.

SVGPNG

On white and off-white. Essential for formal print: letterheads, proposals, reports.

06 · Colour Palette

Colours that
Command Attention

Gold is never decorative — it carries meaning. Use colours precisely and never introduce unapproved tones alongside the Paruto identity. Click a swatch to copy its hex value.

Approved Combinations

Gold on Black — hero pairing
White on Black — high contrast
Black on Off-White — print
Gold gradient — rules, bars, large display only

Never

✕ Introduce unapproved colours
✕ Use the gold logo on white or light backgrounds
✕ Reduce logo opacity below 80% (watermarks excepted)
✕ Place the gold logo on colourful backgrounds

12 · Do's & Don'ts

Protect
the Mark

Never modify the logo in any way not documented here. Always use the supplied files — never recreate or retype the mark.

Do use the supplied gold logo on black.

Don't use gold on white — contrast fails.

Don't stretch or squash the proportions.

Don't rotate or tilt the logo.

Don't recolour in unapproved colours.

Don't place it on colourful backgrounds.

Don't add shadows, glows or effects.

Don't reduce opacity below 80%.
